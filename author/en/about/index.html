About

Understand the problem first.

A mathematician by education. A developer by profession. Curious about how things work.

Born in Moscow in 1973. Graduated from the Moscow Aviation Institute in 1996 in Applied Mathematics, qualifying as a mathematical engineer. Pursued postgraduate studies at MAI and completed professional development in logistics at Plekhanov Russian University of Economics in 2014. Living in Israel since 2022.

I specialize in mathematical optimization of practical production problems. For over 20 years, I have developed packaging software, from understanding requirements to implementation.
